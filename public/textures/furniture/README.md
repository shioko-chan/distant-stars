# Living-room PBR materials

Source: ambientCG by Lennart Demes. License: CC0 1.0 (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/).

- Marble006 (honed dark stone floor): https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Marble006
- Wood048: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood048
- Fabric045: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric045
- Carpet011: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet011

Downloaded the official 2K-JPG archives on 2026-09-19. Retained Color, NormalGL and Roughness maps at 2048×2048, converted to WebP with FFmpeg libwebp quality 92. No displacement or extra geometry. Base color uses sRGB; normal and roughness maps use linear data. Wood tiles cover 0.8 m, fabric 0.5 m, carpet 1 m (latter two are artistic scales). Furniture face UVs are scaled in metres. Runtime tint, normal strength and sofa sheen are adjusted for the dim cabin lighting.

Floor: Marble006, 2K Color/NormalGL/Roughness converted to WebP quality 92. 2.4 m square texture scale avoids stretching veins. Aligned 1.2 × 2.4 m tiles with 4 mm dark grout; runtime roughness remapped to 0.48–0.73 for a honed surface.
